Trading Around Macro Events

FOMC, CPI, GDP, PCE, earnings — these are the days the market moves on a schedule instead of on its own logic, and they're where most of the damage from guessing gets done. The articles below cover how to trade around a known event without predicting the outcome: what to do with implied volatility before the print, where to mark levels in advance, and how the tape actually behaved on specific real dates.

Some of these are evergreen frameworks. Others are dated recaps of a specific day's real price action — kept here because the mechanical lesson from that day still applies to the next one.
